
# Founders School | Head of Student Life

### Interactive 30/60/90-Day Plan

An interactive planning tool outlining a proposed approach to building and managing residential student life at Founders School in New York City.

The plan focuses on creating a welcoming, close-knit community where students can develop friendships, explore individual interests, and enjoy a well-rounded life outside the classroom, while maintaining clear expectations, accountability, and adult supervision.

## The Approach

The plan is built around five guiding balances:

- **Structure & Flexibility:** Establish clear routines and expectations while leaving room for downtime and spontaneity.
- **Individual & Community:** Support each student's interests while building connections through shared experiences.
- **Fun & Responsibility:** Create an enjoyable, supportive environment with clear boundaries and accountability.
- **Ambition & Well-Rounded Life:** Complement the school's entrepreneurial focus with sports, arts, friendships, and new experiences.
- **Leadership & Collaboration:** Take ownership of residential responsibilities while working closely with students and school leadership.

## The 30/60/90-Day Roadmap

### Days 1–30 | Establish

Learn existing school policies and procedures, build individual relationships with students, establish household expectations, introduce family dinners, and identify extracurricular opportunities.

### Days 31–60 | Build

Develop consistent weekly activities, support extracurricular participation, strengthen community traditions, introduce student-led experiences, and improve coordination across residences.

### Days 61–90 | Refine

Gather feedback from students, parents, and staff; improve routines and programming; and document effective practices to support future growth.

## Interactive Features

- **Expandable roadmap:** Explore specific actions, responsibilities, and expected outcomes for each phase.
- **Collaborative planning:** Add notes, adjust responsibilities, and track ideas requiring further discussion.
- **Editable weekly schedule:** Explore how extracurriculars, family dinners, weekend activities, and downtime can fit together.
- **Measures of success:** Evaluate student belonging, engagement, well-being, parent confidence, and operational consistency.
- **Export and import:** Save and transfer planning changes between sessions or collaborators.

## Purpose

This project is intended as a starting point for discussion and collaboration, rather than a fixed operating plan. It provides a practical framework for developing residential life while leaving room for input from Founders School leadership, residential staff, and students.

The overarching goal is simple: **Build a home where ambitious teenagers can be teenagers.**
